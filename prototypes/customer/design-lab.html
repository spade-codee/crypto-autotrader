LIQUIDITY SWEEP · v0 / RESEARCH · NOT AVAILABLE

Wait for the setup.
Then the confirmation.

A proposed sequence to test. These steps are not current market signals, and no performance has been established.

  1. Read the structureUse completed 4-hour candles: the last two confirmed swing highs and lows must rise, and the latest close must stay above the last swing low.
  2. Mark a prior lowStart with the previous UTC day’s low as a possible liquidity area. It does not reveal real stop orders.
  3. Wait for a sweep and reclaimThe day’s first 15-minute candle to dip below that level must close back above it. Otherwise there is no setup that day.
  4. Wait for confirmationWithin the next eight candles, a close must clear a reference swing high known before the sweep candle opened. A lower sweep low or failed structure cancels the setup first; otherwise it expires.
  5. Check the trade conditionsEntry, position size, protective exit, fees and slippage must meet rules fixed before any testing. If they don’t, skip.
Research comes first. This candidate requires its own sizing and exit behaviour; it cannot simply share control of the daily strategy’s whole account.
Will it run automatically? Can I adjust it?

The plan is to automate setup detection, confirmation, entry and exits. It is not available yet. Proposed customization starts with bounded risk settings and tested presets. Changes to entry or exit rules need a separate configuration and fresh testing; there are no editable settings in this preview.
