Wait for the setup.
Then the confirmation.

A proposed sequence to test. These steps are not current market signals, and no performance has been established.

  1. Read the structureUse completed higher-timeframe candles to identify confirmed rising swing highs and lows.
  2. Mark a prior lowStart with the previous day’s low as a possible liquidity area. It does not reveal real stop orders.
  3. Wait for a sweep and reclaimPrice trades below that level, then a candle closes back above it.
  4. Wait for confirmationA later candle must close above a swing high identified before the sweep. Otherwise the setup expires.
  5. Check the trade conditionsEntry, position size, protective exit, fees and slippage must satisfy the tested rules. If they don’t, skip.
Research comes first. This candidate requires its own sizing and exit behaviour; it cannot simply share control of the daily strategy’s whole account.
